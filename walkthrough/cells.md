# Look inside a cell

Double-click a cell or press Enter.

- JSON text opens as a tree you can fold.
- PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP BLOBs show as pictures.
- Float32 and float64 BLOBs show dimension, min, max, mean and L2 norm.
- Every BLOB shows its size and a hex dump.

Press Escape to close the viewer.
